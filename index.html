Who we are

Operators, not advisors.

Shalev Group is a private operating group in waste-to-value. We bring the feedstock, the technology, the site and the capital together, and stay until the plant runs.

Our scope

From end of life to feedstock.

End-of-life tyres. Plastics and refuse-derived fuel. Landfills and legacy waste. Medical and electronic waste. And the digital growth engine that finds the demand for all of it.

Our values

Nothing wasted.

Integrity before introductions. Evidence before promises. Tonnes, permits and offtake checked before anyone builds.

Our mission

Bring the feedstock, the technology, the site and the capital together. Then see it through.

Our vision

Every tyre, every tonne of plastic, every landfill turned back into fuel, carbon and energy, where it lies.
